Judith Capabilities

Ask About Bills
• Ask what’s due this week.
• Ask when the next bill is due.
• Ask what bills are overdue.
• Ask how much is still unpaid this month.
• Ask the total due for the month.
• Ask the estimated total for next month.
• Ask the biggest bill.
• Ask which bill needs attention first.
• Ask whether a specific bill was paid.

Example asks:
• “What’s due this week?”
• “When’s my next due date?”
• “How much do I still owe this month?”
• “What’s my biggest bill?”
• “Did I pay Meralco?”
• “Which bill should I pay first?”

Add Bills
• Add a bill by typing in the app.
• Add a bill by voice during setup.
• Add provider, category, amount, due date, frequency, reminder timing, and bill type.
• Track monthly or annual bills.
• Add rent, mortgage, electricity, water, internet, mobile, subscriptions, streaming, web apps, insurance, credit cards, loans, and custom bills.
• Tag bills as personal, business, or by property/home.
• Link recurring charges to a credit card so they are not double-counted.

Example lines:
• “Add Meralco, 3,200 pesos, due every 20th.”
• “Add Netflix, 549 pesos, due on the 15th.”
• “Add rent, 25,000 pesos, due on the 1st.”
• “Add my BPI credit card, 12,400 pesos, due on June 28.”
• “This Spotify bill is charged to my credit card.”

Update Bills
• Edit provider, amount, category, due day, frequency, reminder schedule, bill type, property tag, and business tag.
• Update variable bills when the amount changes.
• Update credit card statement amounts.
• Record partial payments.
• Mark bills as paid.
• Delete bills no longer tracked.

Example lines:
• “Update Meralco to 3,850 pesos.”
• “Change my internet due date to the 12th.”
• “Mark Netflix as paid.”
• “I paid 2,000 pesos toward my credit card.”
• “Delete my old Globe bill.”

Reminders
• Get reminders before bills are due.
• Choose how many days before due date Judith reminds you.
• Choose reminder time of day.
• Get overdue nudges.
• Get credit card statement update nudges.
• Use notification actions like marking paid or reminding tomorrow.

Example lines:
• “Remind me three days before Meralco is due.”
• “Remind me in the morning.”
• “Remind me tomorrow.”
• “Mark this paid.”
• “Snooze this bill until tomorrow.”

Planning And Forecasting
• See what’s coming before payday.
• Estimate next month’s total.
• Ask what will be left after bills if income is set.
• Ask practical affordability questions.
• Understand fixed vs variable monthly obligations.
• Avoid double-counting bills charged through tracked credit cards.

Example asks:
• “What bills are coming up before payday?”
• “How much will I have left after bills?”
• “Can I afford to go on vacation next month?”
• “What are my fixed bills every month?”
• “How much are subscriptions costing me?”
