Voice Capabilities

1. Ask what’s due
Judith can tell users what bills are coming up soon, what’s due this week, and when the next due date is.

2. Ask how much they owe
Users can ask for this month’s total, remaining unpaid bills, or totals by category like utilities, rent, credit cards, or subscriptions.

3. Ask what’s already paid
Judith can answer whether a specific bill looks paid or still unpaid.

4. Ask what’s biggest or most urgent
Users can ask which bill is largest, which one is closest to due, or what needs attention first.

5. Ask about credit cards
Judith can total credit card bills, track current statement balances, and account for recurring charges linked to cards.

6. Ask about next month
Judith can estimate next month’s bills based on the bills already tracked.

7. Ask what’s left after bills
If income is set, users can ask how much money may be left after upcoming bills.

8. Ask affordability questions
Judith can answer practical “can I afford this?” questions using tracked bills and expected upcoming totals.

9. Add bills by talking
During setup or bill entry flows, users can tell Judith the provider, amount, and due date instead of typing everything manually.

10. Hear answers spoken back
With Voice Ask, Judith can reply out loud so users can ask and listen hands-free.

11. Choose Judith’s personality
Users can hear answers in different Judith personas, like professional, funny, caring/mama-style, sarcastic, or bold.

12. Use voice in natural language
Users do not need exact commands. They can ask casually, the way they would ask a person.
